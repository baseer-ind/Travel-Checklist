Delete your Baggo data

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Baggo (the travel-checklist app, package app.arusa.baggo.twa) is run by Ataul Baseer, Hyderabad, India. This page explains how to delete your Baggo data, what is deleted, and how long anything is kept. Questions: hello@arusa.app.

Delete your whole account (and all its data)

You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app, at any time:

  1. Open Baggo and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and go to your account / settings.
  3. Tap “Delete my account” and confirm.

This permanently removes your account and everything tied to it: your display name, your email address (if you signed in with Google), your trips, lists and items, your emergency contacts, and your family/group membership. You are signed out immediately and the data cannot be recovered.

Delete specific things (without deleting your account)

  • A trip and all its lists: open the trip and tap the trash / Delete trip icon.
  • An item on a list: remove it from the list.
  • An emergency contact: open your contacts and tap Delete next to it.

Anything you create only on your device (before signing in) is stored on that device and is removed when you delete it in the app or clear the app’s data in your phone settings.

Prefer us to do it? Request deletion by email

If you can’t sign in, email hello@arusa.app from the address linked to your account (or tell us your Baggo display name), with the subject “Delete my Baggo data”. We’ll verify it’s your account and delete it.

What is kept, and for how long

When you delete your account, your data is removed from our live database straight away. We do not keep a profile of you, and we do not sell or share your data. Residual copies in routine encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days. We keep nothing that identifies you after that. We may retain non-identifying, aggregate counts (for example, how many accounts exist) that cannot be traced back to you.
