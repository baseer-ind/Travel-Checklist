Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Baggo (“we”, “the app”) is a travel-checklist app that lets you and the people you travel with get ready for a trip together. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. It is written to be read, not to hide things.

1. Who runs Baggo

Baggo is run by Ataul Baseer, Hyderabad, India. Contact: __CONTACT_EMAIL__. Our grievance contact for data questions is Ataul Baseer, at the same email.

2. What we collect

You can use Baggo with no account at all — in that mode your trips live only on your device and we collect nothing on our servers.

If you create an account, we store: your chosen display name and your password (kept only as a salted scrypt hash — we never store or see your actual password); your trips, lists and items, including travel dates, destinations you type, notes and links you add; and your family/group membership (the shared code you join with) and your activity in a shared circle (for example, “packed Jacket”).

Apart from emergency contacts you choose to add (below), we do not collect your email, your location, payment or card details, or government IDs. We do not run third-party advertising or tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Emergency contacts. Baggo lets you store emergency contacts — names and phone numbers — as your own private data, kept in your account like your lists. They are private to you: they are never shown to your family, your group, the admin, or anyone with a sharing code, and they are only shared when you choose to share them (for example, via your phone’s share sheet or a wallet card you print). You can download them in your data export and remove them by deleting the contact or your account.

3. How we use your information

Only to run the app: to save your lists, sync them across your devices, and — if you join a family or group — show the other members the lists and progress you chose to share. We do not run third-party advertising or analytics.

4. Who can see what

You see everything in your own account. Your family/group members and the group’s admin see your shared lists, progress and activity. Items, lists or trips you mark private (🙈) are hidden from everyone except you — they do not appear in any family view, admin page, share link or activity feed, and nobody is told something is hidden. The person who operates Baggo has technical access to the database and could in principle read stored data, including private items (private items are hidden in the app, not encrypted at rest); we access data only to run and support the service.

5. Where it is stored and security

On your device (in your browser), and — if you sign in — on our hosting provider’s servers: Vercel, with an Upstash Redis database. Data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS, and your password is stored only as a salted scrypt hash.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete the item or delete your account. You can export all of your data as a JSON file from Settings at any time, and you can permanently delete your account and all of its data from Settings; deletion is permanent and completes within 24 hours. If you clear your browser data without an account, your trips are gone — we have no copy.

7. Children

Baggo is intended for people aged 16 and older, and we do not knowingly collect the data of children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to withdraw consent. Use the export/delete tools in Settings, or contact us at the email above.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
